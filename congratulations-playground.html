Shared behavior

The first remaining life protects the run but earns no multiplier. Each heart above one adds 10% to the score sequentially, so three lives are ×1.10 ×1.10 = ×1.21. The displayed score rounds to the nearest whole point after each pulse.

  • Every entrance, heart bonus, and confetti treatment has its own manual replay button.
  • “Full sequence” is the only control that chains the three phases.
  • Reduced motion preserves score and bonus information, but applies it immediately and replaces particle motion with a brief static celebration.
  • The share card remains recognizable from v6; celebration is concentrated around the result, not the utility controls.
