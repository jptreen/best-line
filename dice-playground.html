REFERENCE TOKENS

Recommendation

Faceted is the strongest default. The inner geometry helps d10, d12, and d20 feel like physical polyhedral dice, while their outer silhouettes stay legible in peripheral vision.

Accessibility rule

Colour is always redundant. A player can identify every die from shape plus its visible d-number, including in monochrome or reduced-contrast conditions.

The die body, opacity, and d-label stay fixed. Compare four roll-number sizes across every silhouette.
